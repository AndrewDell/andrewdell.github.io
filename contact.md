# Contact

The fastest way to reach me is a message on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-p-dell). I read every message myself and usually reply within a few days.

## Good reasons to get in touch

- **Support operations and AI in support.** Questions about running AI agents next to a human team, knowledge bases that an AI can actually use, handing conversations from AI to people at the right moment, or Zendesk and Botpress setups.
- **Roles.** I'm interested in customer experience and support operations roles, from analyst to architect to director, especially remote roles or companies with travel benefits.
- **Speaking and podcasts.** I've presented on AI in support with the CX Network and I'm glad to talk about full coverage, not full automation.
- **Playbooks.** If you used one of the [free playbooks](/playbooks/) and have feedback or a correction, I'd like to hear it.
- **Small-business help.** If you run a small business and want free mentoring, request a mentor through [SCORE](https://www.score.org/fl/jacksonville/mentors/andrew-dell/), where I volunteer in Jacksonville.
