# Privacy

This is a personal website with free resources. It is built to collect as little as possible.

## What this site collects

- **No analytics or tracking.** There are no analytics scripts, advertising tags, tracking pixels or third-party embeds.
- **No cookies.** The only thing stored in your browser is your light or dark theme choice, saved in local storage on your own device if you use the Theme button. It never leaves your browser.
- **No forms or accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for and no way to submit personal information on this site.
- **Downloads are open.** Playbooks are free to read and download without giving an email address.

## What the host logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). I don't receive or use those logs.

## Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, SCORE and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

## AI crawlers and reuse

Search engines and AI crawlers are allowed to read this site, as stated in [robots.txt](/robots.txt). Playbooks are licensed CC BY 4.0 and Claude skill files are MIT licensed, so you're free to reuse them with credit.

For any privacy question, message me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-p-dell).
